The scheme

Warm Mediterranean, on an everyday budget

Sea light does most of the work. Walls are a warm, chalky white; one light oak floor runs unbroken through every dry room, so the hall, kitchen and living room read as one open space and the 87 m² feel larger.

Colour comes from the earth, not from paint charts: a clay limewash behind the TV, sage behind the main bed and on the kitchen fronts, terracotta and ochre in the textiles, terracotta tiles outside. Texture does the rest: linen, jute, rattan, cane and glazed zellige-look tiles.

Everything shown is a standard, widely available kind of piece. The only made-to-measure item is the kitchen, and it is flat-pack with painted fronts.

Where to save, where to spend

  • Spend on the sofa, the mattress and the kitchen fronts: they are touched every day.
  • Save on dining chairs, lamps, rugs and baskets: mid-range versions look the same in the room.
  • One floor everywhere costs less to lay and makes the flat feel bigger.
  • Limewash two walls instead of tiling or panelling them: the warmest effect for the least money.
  • Zellige-look ceramic gives the handmade look of real zellige at a fraction of the price.
